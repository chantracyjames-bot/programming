- Information Management
    - follows the IPO principle
        - Input
            - contains raw facts and data
        - Process
            - is the methods that converts data into information
        - Output
            - information
    - Knowledge vs Wisdom
        - Knowledge
            - contains information
            - available to decision makers
                - or available to utilize for actions
        - Wisdom
            - it is the information that is applied
