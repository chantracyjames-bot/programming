    - Architecture and Organization     
        - Architecture
            - is the schematics, planneing, foundation, structure, and interior
            - Functional System Design
                - of a computer
        - Organization
            - is the consolidation, interconnectedness, management, arrangement and group
            - Network of interconnected of devices
                - through connectivity, wired or wireless
        - Relationship between Arch and Org
            - the Organization is called into use by the Architecture
                - the Architecture sits inside the computer
                - the Organization is what is plugged into a computer
            - i.e. when a computer formed by the Architecture needs the connected buses of the Organization
        - CISC
            - complex instruction set computers
            - carries out complex instruction sets
            - drawbacks:
                - difficulty
            - advantages:
                - speed
        - RISC
            - reduced instruction set computers
            - drawbacks:
                - speed (slow)
            - advantages:
                - simple
